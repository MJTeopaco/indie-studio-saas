# Client Brief: Target Respondent List for StudioSprint Data Gathering

**Project:** StudioSprint – AI-Powered Project Management & Resource Scheduling SaaS  
**Purpose:** User Testing, Data Gathering, & Usability Evaluation  
**Target Total Respondents:** ~50 – 70 Participants (Across 5 – 10 Studio Teams)

---

To gather comprehensive data for our evaluation, we need participants who match the **4 core role categories** defined inside the system:

---

## 👥 Breakdown by Role Category

### 1. Management & Project Leads (Target: 10 – 15 Pax)
* **Who to Invite:** Studio Owners, Founders, Project Managers (PMs), Technical Product Managers, Scrum Masters.
* **What They Will Test:**
  * AI-assisted sprint planning & automated task decomposition.
  * Project timeline Gantt charts & critical path schedule breach alerts.
  * Team capacity & overall resource allocation boards.

---

### 2. Core Software & Game Developers (Target: 25 – 35 Pax)
* **Who to Invite:** Full Stack, Backend, Frontend, Mobile, and Game Developers (Unity / Unreal / Godot).
* **What They Will Test:**
  * Developer Passport onboarding (rating skills across 96 categories).
  * Personal Kanban task boards & AI-calculated developer-to-task match scores (e.g., "94% Fit").
  * AI agentic chat interface for task updates.

---

### 3. Technical Leads & Specialized Engineers (Target: 15 – 20 Pax)
* **Who to Invite:** Solutions Architects, Tech Leads, AI / ML Engineers, DevOps / DevSecOps, QA Automation Engineers, and Hardware/Embedded Engineers.
* **What They Will Test:**
  * Accuracy of the Graph Neural Network (GNN) matching algorithm.
  * Developer capacity validation & double-booking protection.
  * Specialized technical skill matrix granularity.

---

### 4. Product, Design & Business Analysts (Target: 10 – 15 Pax)
* **Who to Invite:** Business Analysts, Product Design Engineers, UI/UX Designers, Research Analysts.
* **What They Will Test:**
  * Natural language AI intent parser (converting text briefs into structured tasks).
  * System usability, layout ergonomics, and design clarity.

---

## 📝 What Participants Will Be Asked to Do

Each participant will complete a short, 15-to-20-minute session consisting of:
1. **Hands-On Task Trial (10 mins):** Complete 2–3 routine tasks on the platform matching their role.
2. **Short Survey (5 mins):** Fill out a brief System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire.
